PROJECT FOOT MONEYBALL FOR macOS

1. Double-click Project Foot Moneyball.app.
2. Leave the ADP box blank to reuse the included snapshot, or paste a current ADP comparison URL.
3. Click Update Data.
4. Click Open Excel when it finishes.

The first time you open the app, macOS may ask you to confirm that you trust it.
If it is blocked, Control-click the app, choose Open, then choose Open again.

Your downloaded data, rankings, and spreadsheet are saved in:
Documents/Project Foot Moneyball

Internet access is required when downloading new stats or ADP.
