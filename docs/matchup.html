    How this works

    The offense chooses a play using the game situation and observed tendencies. The defense supplies pressure and coverage tendencies; available players and past outcomes influence the result. That one result updates the scoreboard, offensive players and D/ST together.

    Head coach, offensive coordinator, defensive coordinator and verified play caller are tracked separately. Detailed coverage is prior-season evidence, not a claim that a particular cornerback will cover a particular receiver. Rain, snow and altitude do not yet have separate effects.
