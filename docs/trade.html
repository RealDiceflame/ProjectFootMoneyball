          How to read these results

          Rest-of-season roster impact chooses the best projected eligible starters separately each week, including scheduled byes and recorded out games. This is expected lineup potential, not guaranteed scoring. Unknown projections stop the calculation instead of becoming zeros.

          Full-season and dynasty roster comparisons use one illustrative lineup across the chosen horizon. Dynasty combines simulated remaining games with two age-adjusted, discounted full-season scenarios; future teams, coaches, roles and schedules are not known. Read the Player Value chart for individual game forecasts and source notes.

          Each side may contain up to eight players. Selections save on this browser only. No trade is sent, no league account is connected, and your draft-board selections are unchanged. Projection accuracy remains experimental.
