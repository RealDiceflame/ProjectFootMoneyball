Sources & data notes

Team and player statistics: nflverse, CC BY 4.0. Selected and formatted by OutlierBaseline. Team marks belong to their respective owners.

Box scores usually arrive after games and may be corrected later. A dash means unavailable, not zero. These releases do not include possession time, third/fourth-down conversion rates, snap counts or play-by-play. Player rows cover recorded statistics, not the full active roster. Anonymous source events are kept separate from named players.

Data is archived by season and game for future research. These are observed results, not predictions; preserving them does not automatically retrain a model.

Weather: reported stadium conditions from nflverse or saved National Weather Service kickoff forecasts. Forecasts are not observations; indoor venues are marked separately. Future forecasts generally become available within seven days. Weather may be unavailable for international venues.
