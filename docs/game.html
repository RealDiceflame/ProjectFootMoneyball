Sources & data notes

Team and player statistics: nflverse, CC BY 4.0. Selected and formatted by OutlierBaseline. Team marks belong to their respective owners.

Box scores usually arrive after games and may be corrected later. A dash means unavailable, not zero. The box-score feed does not supply possession time or third/fourth-down conversion rates. Snap counts and 2025 regular-season play-by-play come from separate datasets shown above. Player rows cover recorded statistics, not the full active roster. Anonymous source events are kept separate from named players.

Regular-season data is archived by season and game for future research. Historical archives may receive source corrections; their age is not a live-feed outage. Historical franchise codes are preserved and shown instead of modern team marks. These are observed results, not predictions; preserving them does not automatically retrain a model.

Weather: reported stadium conditions from nflverse or saved National Weather Service kickoff forecasts. Forecasts are not observations; indoor venues are marked separately. Future forecasts generally become available within seven days. Weather may be unavailable for international venues.
