How the calculator works

    ADP comes from the same saved market data as the rankings, or your imported ADP on this device. Missing ADP stays unknown. The simulator supplies projected fantasy points; the calculator adds roster rules and a clearly labeled draft scenario. No league account is connected and no picks are submitted elsewhere.

    Kickers use 3 points per field goal and 1 per extra point. D/ST uses OB’s full-scoreboard points-allowed scoring. Draft projections assume current roles across the full schedule; future availability remains conditional.
