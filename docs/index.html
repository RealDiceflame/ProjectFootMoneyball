Sources & privacy

Scores: Lee Sharpe / nflverse, CC BY 4.0. Selected and formatted by OutlierBaseline. Reported scores are not a live game clock or confirmation of a final result. Checks run about every 15 minutes near games and every six hours otherwise; source and publishing delays can occur.

Injuries: nflverse. Covered fantasy players only; a missing report does not mean healthy. Questionable, Probable and practice participation alone do not earn a Risk label. News: ESPN NFL RSS, with Yahoo Sports RSS as a fallback. News, injury and game-stat collection runs every six hours; this page checks for saved updates each minute. Team marks belong to their respective owners.

The NFL feed connects to X, which may receive browsing information and use cookies. X privacy information. OutlierBaseline is independent and not affiliated with the featured platforms.

Game weather: reported stadium temperature and wind from nflverse, or a saved National Weather Service kickoff forecast. Forecasts refresh with the six-hour data update and remain labeled as forecasts after games. Missing weather stays unavailable; indoor venues are marked separately.
