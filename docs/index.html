Sources & privacy

Scores from Lee Sharpe / nflverse, adapted by OutlierBaseline under CC BY 4.0. Reported scores may be delayed and are not confirmation of a final result.

Injury reports from nflverse cover selected fantasy players. Missing reports do not confirm health. Questionable, Probable and practice participation alone do not trigger Risk. News comes from ESPN, with Yahoo Sports as a backup.

Scores are checked more often near games; news, injuries and game stats are collected every six hours. Delays can occur. Weather comes from nflverse or National Weather Service forecasts. Forecasts remain labeled as forecasts.
