Sources & data notes

Recorded regular-season statistics from nflverse (CC BY 4.0), selected and summarized by OutlierBaseline. Box scores may arrive late or be corrected. These are not live play-by-play statistics.

Leaders use available box scores only, with season totals or a single week's results. Players are matched by unique ID, including players who change teams. Zero totals and anonymous team events are omitted. Equal values share a rank; alphabetical order breaks a tie at the ten-player display limit. Longest kicks and punts use the maximum, not the sum. Rate statistics are not included.

Fantasy totals use the source's standard or PPR scoring, not your league's custom scoring. A reported score is not a confirmed final-status indicator. Team marks belong to their respective owners.
